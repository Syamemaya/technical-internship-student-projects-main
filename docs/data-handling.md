# Data handling boundary

> 已确认：学生作业仓、评分和答辩材料在最终验收后保留 30 个自然日，由当前管理员执行删除。

Do not commit real resumes, interview notes, student numbers, contact
information, credentials, private datasets or talent-pool decisions.

Formal cohort records must stay in an institution-approved system and follow
the institution's access, retention and deletion policy. Use pseudonymous
assignment IDs and keep the mapping outside Git. The operational deletion
procedure is documented in [data retention and deletion policy](data-retention.md).
